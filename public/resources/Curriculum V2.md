**Preface**

Welcome to the comprehensive curriculum description of the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering (B.Sc. in CSE) degree program offered by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering under the Faculty of Engineering at Exim Bank Agricultural University Bangladesh. In an era characterized by unprecedented technological advancement and digital transformation, we are privileged to present a meticulously designed curriculum that seamlessly integrates the theoretical foundations of computer science with the practical applications of computer engineering.

This four-year undergraduate engineering program has been developed in accordance with the principles of Outcome-Based Education (OBE), emphasizing the cultivation of competencies, skills, and professional attributes essential for success in the contemporary technological landscape. Our curriculum is strategically crafted to prepare graduates who can effectively navigate and contribute to the Fourth Industrial Revolution while addressing the unique challenges and opportunities within Bangladesh's evolving technological ecosystem.

The field of Computer Science and Engineering stands at the intersection of innovation and application, where abstract computational theories converge with tangible engineering solutions. From fundamental algorithmic thinking to advanced artificial intelligence systems, from secure network architectures to sustainable computing practices, our program encompasses the full spectrum of contemporary computing disciplines. Students will engage with cutting-edge areas, including Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Internet of Things (IoT), Mobile Application Development, and emerging paradigms in software engineering.

Our curriculum architecture thoughtfully balances rigorous theoretical instruction with extensive practical experience. Students will explore comprehensive subject areas spanning discrete mathematics, digital logic design, programming fundamentals, data structures and algorithms, computer architecture, operating systems, database management systems, computer networks, software engineering methodologies, artificial intelligence, computer graphics, mobile computing, and information security. Additionally, the program integrates essential components of engineering ethics, project management, entrepreneurship development, and the historical and cultural context of Bangladesh's technological progress.

The program is delivered by a distinguished faculty comprising accomplished researchers, industry practitioners, and educational leaders who bring both academic excellence and real-world expertise to the classroom. Through their guidance, students will develop not only technical proficiency but also critical thinking skills, innovative problem-solving abilities, and the capacity to translate complex theoretical concepts into practical engineering solutions.

Throughout their academic journey, students will participate in collaborative projects, industry partnerships, research initiatives, and comprehensive capstone experiences designed to simulate real-world engineering challenges. These experiential learning opportunities ensure that graduates are well-prepared to make immediate and meaningful contributions to various sectors of the technology industry upon completion of their studies.

At Exim Bank Agricultural University Bangladesh, we are committed to fostering a learning environment that emphasizes research excellence, innovation, and social responsibility. Our educational philosophy extends beyond technical competency to encompass leadership development, effective communication, teamwork, ethical reasoning, and entrepreneurial thinking. Following OBE principles, we aim to develop well-rounded engineering professionals who possess both the technical expertise and the professional maturity to excel in diverse career paths and contribute positively to society.

The contemporary landscape of Computer Science and Engineering offers abundant opportunities for professional growth and specialization. Our graduates are prepared to assume roles as software engineers, systems architects, data scientists, cybersecurity specialists, artificial intelligence engineers, product managers, technology consultants, research scientists, and entrepreneurial leaders. The comprehensive skill set acquired through this program positions our graduates to drive innovation, solve complex problems, and create technological solutions that enhance the quality of life and promote sustainable development.

We invite our students to embark on this transformative educational experience as they explore the frontiers of computational science, engineering design, emerging technologies, and interdisciplinary applications. Through commitment to academic excellence, intellectual curiosity, and professional development, our students will undoubtedly contribute to shaping a future where technology catalyzes positive change and societal advancement.

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Exim Bank Agricultural University Bangladesh is dedicated to supporting each student's journey toward becoming a competent, ethical, and innovative engineering professional ready to meet the challenges of tomorrow's technological landscape.

**10\. Description of the program:**  
The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering (B.Sc. in CSE) at _Exim Bank Agricultural University Bangladesh_ is a four-year undergraduate program designed to prepare graduates with strong foundations in computing, engineering principles, and problem-solving skills. The program integrates theoretical knowledge with extensive laboratory work, design projects, and industry exposure to ensure that students are capable of addressing complex engineering problems in real-world contexts.

The curriculum is structured under the _Outcome-Based Education (OBE)_ framework, ensuring constructive alignment between course learning outcomes, program outcomes, and the graduate attributes of the _Washington Accord_. It emphasizes not only technical competence but also research, innovation, ethical practice, sustainability, teamwork, communication, and lifelong learning — in line with the _Bangladesh National Qualifications Framework (BNQF Level 7\)_.

Graduates of this program will be able to design and develop software systems, manage large-scale data, apply artificial intelligence and emerging technologies, and contribute to the advancement of digital transformation both nationally and globally. The program also fosters entrepreneurial mindsets and leadership qualities, enabling graduates to excel in industry, research, or higher studies, while making meaningful contributions to society.

3. **Range of Washington Accord Complex Engineering Problem Solving (WP):**

|   WP1   | Depth of knowledge required: Cannot be resolved without in-depth engineering knowledge at the level of one or more of WK3, WK4, WK5, WK6, or WK8, which allows a fundamentals-based, first principles analytical approach. |
| :-----: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **WP2** | **Range of conflicting requirements:** Involve wide-ranging or conflicting technical, engineering, and other issues.                                                                                                       |
| **WP3** | **Depth of analysis required:** Have no obvious solution and require abstract thinking and originality in analysis to formulate suitable models.                                                                           |
| **WP4** | **Familiarity of issues:** Involve infrequently encountered issues                                                                                                                                                         |
| **WP5** | **Extent of applicable codes:** Outside problems encompassed by standards and codes of practice for professional engineering.                                                                                              |
| **WP6** | **Extent of stakeholder involvement and needs:** Involve diverse groups of stakeholders with widely varying needs.                                                                                                         |
| **WP7** | **Interdependence:** High-level problems including many components or sub-problems.                                                                                                                                        |
